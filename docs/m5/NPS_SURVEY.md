# NPS Survey (M5)

Ask in person or on a call, at week 4, after the interview. Ask exactly this —
do not paraphrase, do not explain the scale, do not lead.

---

**"On a scale of 0 to 10, how likely are you to recommend Home Hub to a
friend or family member?"**

(0 = not at all likely, 10 = extremely likely)

Then, whatever the score:

**"What's the main reason for your score?"**

Write down the answer verbatim. Do not suggest reasons.

---

## Scoring

- 9–10 → Promoter
- 7–8 → Passive
- 0–6 → Detractor

**NPS = % Promoters − % Detractors** (a number from −100 to +100).
Gate target: **NPS ≥ 40**.
